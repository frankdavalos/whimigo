Installing the Arcade

In a browser tab, Safari may clear Progress after a week without a visit. Installed, the Arcade keeps it. On the iPad:

  1. Tap Share.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  4. Open Whimigo from the Home Screen.
